About Justin Hart

Savannah real estate, seen through a designer's eye.

Justin Hart is an Associate Broker with Seabolt Real Estate in Savannah, Georgia, specializing in the Historic District and the neighborhoods stretching south of it. Before real estate, Justin built a career in high-end renovation and construction management, overseeing complex projects in Los Angeles, New York, and later with a builder in Bluffton, South Carolina. That hands-on background is something most agents simply don't bring to the table.

When Justin walks through a property, he sees it differently — what it needs, what it doesn't, and what buyers will actually pay for the updates a seller is considering. Whether you're buying a century-old rowhouse in the Historic District or selling a bungalow in Ardsley Park, that design and construction fluency translates into sharper pricing, smarter negotiations, and fewer surprises at inspection.

Justin has been selling Savannah real estate since 2010, the year he made the city his permanent home. He lives in Ardsley Park — one of Savannah's beloved early-20th-century neighborhoods — and has the kind of street-level knowledge of the market that only comes from spending over a decade genuinely embedded in a place. Originally from Memphis, he brings a straightforward, unpretentious approach to what can otherwise be a stressful process.

When he's not working, you'll likely find Justin on an evening walk through the neighborhood with his dogs, Luke Duke and Rosco.
